One front page summing up world news of **Wednesday 18 November 2026**:
36 stories, 6 continents. Hero: Xi greets Trump in Shenzhen ahead of the
first US–China–Russia trilateral; panels on the Gulf drone strikes, the
Kerch bridge collapse, the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan, COP31, Voyager 1's
one-light-day milestone, and the US losing measles-free status — plus a
29-item index (stories 8–36) and a day-in-numbers strip.

`main.py` (Python 3.13 stdlib only) renders `entry.png` (1024×1024) with
Pillow when available and otherwise copies the bundled `entry.png`
byte-for-byte, so both runs are deterministic. Run:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Sources: Reuters, AP, NASA, European Commission, UN News — 18 Nov 2026.
Disputed claims are attributed; no speculation is shown as fact.
